What I agreed to do on my calls

“After answering, ask ‘Did that answer your question?’”

Goal: 5 of 5 calls · Checked by: Remote Call Listening, 5 calls · Review: Fri, Oct 16

Listen fully, acknowledge what the member said, respond to it, then ask to confirm it landed. Sounds like: “So the claim from your March visit was denied and nobody told you why. Let me pull it up.” … “Did that answer your question?”

How to do it on the call
  1. Let the member finish without interrupting.
  2. Acknowledge what they said in one sentence, using their words.
  3. Respond to that exact point, in plain language.
  4. Ask a check question to confirm it landed.

Avoid: Talking over the member to get to the account faster.; Reading plan codes or terms without explaining them.
