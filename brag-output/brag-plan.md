# Brag Plan: PULSE

## What is this app?
PULSE is coaching intelligence for contact center supervisors: it tells a supervisor **who** to coach, on **which of the 7 core behaviors**, **how** to coach it (DIRECT + the right coaching tool), and **whether it worked**, then follows up every week until the SMART commitment sticks.

## The angle
Every supervisor asks the same four questions every Monday. PULSE answers each one on screen with its own working UI. The video is built as four numbered questions (01–04) asked in the hook, then answered one by one by the real product, then checked off in the outro. The questions come straight from the README/login copy ("Know who to coach, on which of the 7 core behaviors, how to coach it with DIRECT, and whether the SMART commitment stuck"), so the structure is the product's own promise.

Language: all narrative copy on screen is **Spanish** (user request). The product UI is shown as it really is (English labels, fictional demo data). Spanish captions carry the story; the UI is the proof.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Four questions slam in one by one on the beat, big display type, numbered in mono:
`01 ¿A quién coacheo?` · `02 ¿En qué conducta?` · `03 ¿Cómo lo coacheo?` · `04 ¿Funcionó?`
then a quiet line: "Las cuatro preguntas de todo supervisor."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **¿A quién?** The real supervisor dashboard: KPI tiles all marked "Below goal", then the camera pushes into **"Who to coach first"** (ranked by gap to goal). Cursor lands on Luis Ortega: `Outlier` + `No commitment`.
- **¿En qué?** The call-listening grid: ✓ / ✕ marks pop in call by call; the **Set Clear Expectations** row turns red with `✕ 3`. The pick is **Locked in**, then the yellow **AI second opinion** card slides in: "Agrees with you: Set Clear Expectations".
- **¿Cómo?** The SMART checker rejects "Raise my CSAT to 90%" (the S tile goes red: "That's a KPI result. Write the on-call action that moves it."), then the real on-call action is typed and all five S-M-A-R-T tiles go green. The four weekly sessions (Mon–Tue / Mon–Tue / Wed–Thu / Fri) land one by one.
- **¿Cómo? (práctica)** Voice roleplay transcript with a fictional member (Frank, frustrated), then **"How it sounded"**: 164 words/min (target 120–165), 2 s to answer, 0 long silences.
- **¿Funcionó?** The agent's own commitment card ("What I agreed to do on my calls") with the four sessions marked **Held** and the **Met** chip; then the manager's **Coaching effectiveness** table (focus KPI before vs. after, per agent).

## Outro / punchline
The four questions come back as a checklist and each gets a teal ✓. Then the PULSE lockup and the closing line: "Cada semana, hasta que el compromiso se sostenga."

## User flow worth showing
Dashboard ("Who to coach first") → Coaching prep (mark 3 calls, lock pick, AI second opinion) → DIRECT session SMART commitment + 4 weekly sessions → roleplay practice with delivery feedback → Friday: commitment met, KPI before vs. after.

All people shown are the app's built-in **fictional demo users** (Carla Peña, Luis Ortega, Daniela Cruz, Frank Delgado, etc., `@pulse.demo` seed data). No real roster, emails or member data. The yellow "Demo mode" banner is removed from the captured UI. Numbers shown are the demo seed's, presented as UI, never as product results.
